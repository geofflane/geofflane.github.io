About

I'm Geoff Lane. I've been a professional software developer since 1999, working across a lot of different technologies and a lot of different places.

Startups in Silicon Valley during the tail end of the dot-com boom, then Chicago. A consulting firm in Milwaukee. Custom software for clients out of Cincinnati. I've lived in Ohio twice, plus Wisconsin, Illinois, Texas, Virginia and California. Now Windsor, Ontario, with my wife, my son, and two cats.

I've never been especially loyal to one language or paradigm — object-oriented and functional, static and dynamic, whatever the problem actually calls for. I like proven tools and I keep an eye on new ones. I prefer Unixy systems. I use Vim because it's awesome.

Away from a keyboard: a small garden, so I have useable skills after the zombie apocalypse. A lot of metal, especially the Swedish kind — At The Gates, Dark Tranquillity, In Flames, Amon Amarth — though Johnny Cash, Coltrane and Dylan get their turns too. Twelve countries so far, and a list of more.
